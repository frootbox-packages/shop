**Shop sync** shows how the copy of the catalog on the website stands.
The website keeps categories, products and images of the shop, so pages
load fast and appear in search, sitemap and navigation. Every five
minutes it takes the changes from the shop.

## Status

While a sync runs, you see since when and, for the whole catalog, how
many products it has seen. Afterwards you see what the last sync changed.
Images the shop does not hand out are counted with the last reason, e.g.
when the shop answers that an image is missing.

## Sync now

**Sync changes** takes what changed in the shop since the last sync.
**Sync the whole catalog** asks the shop for every product again and
removes products it no longer has, e.g. after moving the website. Your
browser runs the sync step by step: keep the page open until it is done.
**Stop** ends it after the current step; the next sync goes on from
there.
